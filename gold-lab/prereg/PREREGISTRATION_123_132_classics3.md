## Rules (long described; short = mirror unless "long only")
- **#123 PINBALL** (Raschke & Connors, *Street Smarts* 1995, "Momentum Pinball"): LBR/RSI = RSI(3) (Wilder) of the 1-day change C-C[-1]. If LBR/RSI(t-1) < 30 -> on day t buy stop at the high of the first session hour, initial stop = low of the first session hour. If the trade is profitable at the 16:00 NY session close it is carried overnight and exited at the next day's 16:00 NY close (stop moved to entry); otherwise exit at the 16:00 close. Short: LBR/RSI > 70, sell stop at first-hour low. (UNCERTAIN: book exits next morning at the trader's discretion; fixed next-day 16:00 used.)
- **#124 ORB60** (Crabel, opening range breakout, day horizon): buy stop at the first session hour high, sell stop at its low (first touched wins, from 09:00 NY), protective stop = the opposite side of the opening range, exit at 16:00 NY.
- **#125 SPECTRAP** (L. Williams, "Specialist Trap"): a breakout day b = close above the highest high of the 10 days before b; then on any of the 3 days after b, a SELL stop at the LOW of day b (the trap: breakout fails). Exit at the day close (17:00 NY day end); protective stop = the highest high since b. Mirror: breakdown below the 10-day lowest low, buy stop at the breakdown-day high. (UNCERTAIN: box length 10 and 3-day window from memory.)
- **#126 ANTI** (L. Raschke, "The Anti"): stochastic %K(7) fast, %D = SMA(10) of %K. Long setup at t-1: %D rising (D[t-1] > D[t-2]) and %K hooked up after a pullback (K[t-2] < K[t-3] and K[t-1] > K[t-2]). Day t: buy stop at H[t-1], stop = L[t-1], exit at the close of day t+2 (3 days) or the stop. Mirror short.
- **#127 TD9_D1** (T. DeMark, TD Sequential setup): buy setup = 9 consecutive daily closes < the close 4 days earlier (bar 9 completes at t-1) -> buy at the open of day t; stop = TD risk level = lowest low of the 9 setup bars minus (that bar's true range); exit at the close of the 4th day (t+3). Mirror for sell setup.
- **#128 TD13_D1** (TD Sequential countdown): after a completed buy setup, countdown counts days with C <= L two days earlier (not necessarily consecutive), starting at setup bar 9; cancelled if a sell setup completes first. Countdown 13 at t-1 -> buy at the open of day t, stop = lowest low of the countdown minus its true range, exit at the close of t+3. Mirror. (13 must also satisfy L13 <= C8 qualifier; if not, it is deferred to the next qualifying bar.)
- **#129 TD9_H4** and **#130 TD13_H4**: the same rules on 4-hour bars (built from hourly bars, anchored at 17:00 NY: 17,21,01,05,09,13), entry at the open of the next 4H bar, exit at the close of the 4th 4H bar (16 hours).
- **#131 ICHIMOKU** (Hosoda, original 9/26/52 on daily): Tenkan = (HH9+LL9)/2, Kijun = (HH26+LL26)/2, cloud = Senkou A=(T+K)/2 and Senkou B=(HH52+LL52)/2 both plotted 26 days ahead. Long when Tenkan crosses above Kijun while the close is above the cloud; enter at the next day's open; exit when Tenkan crosses below Kijun (next open). Short mirror (close below the cloud).
- **#132 KELTNER** (C. Keltner 1960, "ten-day moving average rule"): mid = SMA10 of typical price (H+L+C)/3; bands = mid +/- SMA10 of (H-L). Buy stop at the upper band of t-1 on day t; exit when a daily close is below mid (exit at that day's close). Mirror short at the lower band, exit on a close above mid.
